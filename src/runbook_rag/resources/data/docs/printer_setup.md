# Printers and scanning

## Adding a printer
Open settings and choose Printers. Select "Add printer" and pick the printer from the print server list. The printer names show the floor and the room, for example "F3-R12". Print a test page after adding it.

## Print jobs stay in the queue
Open the print queue, cancel the stuck job and send it again. If the queue does not clear, restart the print spooler by restarting the laptop. If several people cannot print on the same printer, report the printer name to the service desk because the printer itself may be offline.

## Secure printing
Choose "Secure print" in the print dialog and hold the badge to the reader at the printer to release the job. Unreleased jobs are deleted after 24 hours.

## Scanning to email
Place the document on the glass, choose "Scan to email" on the printer screen and sign in with the badge. The scan arrives in your mailbox as a PDF within a few minutes.
