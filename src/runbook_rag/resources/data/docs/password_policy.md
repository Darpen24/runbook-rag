# Password policy and account lock

## Password rules
A password must have at least 12 characters and include letters and numbers. Passwords expire every 180 days. The last five passwords cannot be reused. Do not share a password with colleagues, not even with the service desk.

## Changing a password
Open the self service password page and choose "Change password". Enter the current password and the new password twice. After the change, sign out of all devices and sign in again so that the new password is used everywhere.

## Forgotten password
Choose "Forgot password" on the sign in page. Confirm your identity with the authenticator app or the phone number on file. Then set a new password. If you cannot confirm your identity, the service desk must verify it by video call.

## Locked account
An account is locked after five wrong password attempts. The lock ends by itself after 30 minutes. If you cannot wait, ask the service desk to unlock it after identity verification.
