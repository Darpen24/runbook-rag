# Laptop handover and return

## New laptop
A new laptop is prepared by the service desk and handed over on the first working day. Sign in with the temporary password from the welcome letter and change it at the first login. Install the standard software from the software portal.

## Replacing a defective laptop
Report the defect to the service desk with a short description. If the laptop cannot be repaired within two days, a loan laptop is provided. Back up important files to the company cloud storage before handing over the device. The service desk wipes the old disk.

## Leaving the company
Return the laptop, the docking station, the badge and the headset on the last working day. Hand them over at the service desk on floor 1. The disk is wiped and the device is reused or recycled. A receipt is issued for every returned item.

## Lost or stolen device
Report a lost or stolen laptop to the service desk and to security within 24 hours. The device is locked remotely so that nobody can read the data.
